KernelLab.app in this deb is a signed arm64 Mach-O (soft UI).

Ubuntu CI has no Theos and no iOS SDK, and no Mac worker was connected,
so the package ships app/prebuilt/KernelLab.app. SpringBoard registers
that executable. postinst runs:

  uicache -p /var/jb/Applications/KernelLab.app

only when file(1) or the MH magic says KernelLab is Mach-O. A /bin/sh
launcher is skipped. Respring if the icon is still missing.

The Console tab posix_spawns /var/jb/usr/bin/kernellab and shows stdout.
The Probes tab lists .m/.h filenames under /var/jb/var/kernellab/probes.
It does not compile, run, or generate them. APPLE-SUBMIT stays closed.

To replace the prebuilt with the SwiftUI Theos app, on a Theos machine:

  export THEOS=~/theos
  make -C app clean all
  ./packaging/build.sh

packaging/build.sh prefers a Mach-O bundle under app/.theos, then
app/prebuilt/KernelLab.app. Regenerate the prebuilt without Theos with
app/stub/build-stub.sh (clang + ld64.lld + ldid).
